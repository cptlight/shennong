# Process-selection rules mined from the corpus

2026-11-05T10:16:39-0800

**The question.** Given a plant of a given chemotype, which 炮制 method does two thousand
years of attested practice select for it — and is that selection statistically real, or is
it just how often the word appears?

**What was scanned.** 7,795 corpus chunks containing both a classical process term and
a known drug surface; 62,445 (drug, process, source) records; 328 plants with a documented chemotype.

**Result.** 0 of 172 process×chemotype cells are enriched beyond the
row marginal at FDR 0.1. Each is a *rule the corpus implies*, listed with its lift,
source-weighted support and citations.

No rule survived FDR control. We report that rather than a suggestive table.
