EthnoHack 2026 · Tracks 01 & 02

SHENNONG 神农

Everyone built a system that reads ancient medicine.
We built one that argues with it.

A 1.5 B open model · 940 MiB · runs on a CPU · everything open

The record is unusable for two reasons — neither is data volume

1 · The names don't survive

甘草 · 감초 · Glycyrrhiza uralensis

…and also Liquorice, Mulethi. A 16th-century drug name and a modern gene target never meet.

2 · Nobody checked the chemistry

A preparation instruction is a chemical protocol written in a language no one validated against thermodynamics.

The binding constraint was never data. It was translation, and then verification. We built both.

Track 01 · Paste a passage, get structured knowledge

Every drug carries a character span and a confidence. Extraction is rule- and lexicon-based, so it cannot invent a span. 脾胃虛弱 → dyspepsia.

Track 02 · Type a target, get a ranked shortlist

23,176 plant compounds · ECFP4 vs known ChEMBL actives · Lipinski ADMET · preparation physics · novelty. HMGCR returns Tanimoto 1.000 — the pipeline rediscovers a known active. That's our control.

The part nobody else has

78/203

recorded preparation methods are physically impossible — the compound decomposes at the temperature the text prescribes.

And 6 of them passed every text-based check. The ancient record agreed. The tradition agreed. Physics said no.

A system that only reads texts cannot know that. Ancient consensus is not evidence.

What we can't do — stated before you ask

48.7 %

of plant materia medica resolves. The rest are archaic names absent from every open lexicon.

19 records

is all the Greek and Latin text we hold. We don't claim to decode them.

~0 %

of the corpus is animal, mineral, or manufactured objects — 37.9 %. Counting them as failures was the wrong denominator.

We tested character-similarity fallback as a coverage fix: it recovered 2.9 % and was wrong half the time. Not shipped.

Why it matters

A laptop in a clinic in Yunnan or Kerala can run this offline. No GPU, no cluster, no API. The corpus is enumerated, every claim cites a span, and the licence audit includes the sources we rejected.

The contribution isn't a bigger model. It's a system that links eras — and then checks itself.
