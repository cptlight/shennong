Ask the corpus 問

Answers are assembled from passages in the loaded corpus and every sentence carries a citation. When the corpus cannot support a claim, the system refuses — a refusal is a result, not a failure.

Research use only — not medical advice. SHENNONG is an instrument for reading historical texts. It does not give dosing, diagnostic or self-treatment guidance, and it will refuse questions that ask for it. Dual-use screening is on: preparation and synthesis questions for toxic substances are declined by policy, not by inability.
